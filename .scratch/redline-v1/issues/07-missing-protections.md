# 07: Missing protections

**What to build:** A freelancer sees a separate, unranked list of protections their contract lacks, checked against a fixed checklist: liability cap, payment terms, termination notice (ADR 0004).

**Blocked by:** 05

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The checklist is product data, not decided by the model
- [ ] Missing protections are listed apart from risk flags, unranked, and quote nothing
- [ ] A protection present in the contract is not reported
- [ ] Analysis seam tests with a scripted model: absent items reported, present items not (PRD check 6)
