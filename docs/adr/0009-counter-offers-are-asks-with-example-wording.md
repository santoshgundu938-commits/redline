# 0009. Counter-offers are plain-language asks with example wording

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-16

No competitor in the research drafts counter-offers, possibly because drafting contract language for one person's contract looks like legal advice. Each counter-offer is a plain-language ask ("ask the client to cap your liability at the project fee") plus example wording labelled as a starting point. Redline never tells the freelancer whether to sign and never claims a clause or its replacement is enforceable.

## Considered options

- **Paste-ready replacement clauses**: the strongest version of the feature, and the most exposed to legal-advice rules.
- **Plain-language asks with no wording**: safest, but drops the feature the research found no one else offers.

## Consequences

- A lawyer reviews this framing before launch; rules on legal advice vary by state and have not been checked.
- Freelancers who want paste-ready clauses must adapt the example wording themselves.
